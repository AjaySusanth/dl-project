## Results summary

The aggregated E2 comparison showed that GRU with a 24-hour lookback had
lower mean validation and test MAE than GRU with a 168-hour lookback and the
E1 LSTM comparison. Its test coverage was approximately `0.701`, within the
expected plausible range.

## Learning summary

E1 compares recurrent cell types under the same data and training protocol.
E2 tests how much recent history is useful. A shorter lookback performed
better here, suggesting that the most recent daily pattern was more useful
than the full weekly history for this dataset. This is an observed result,
not a general claim that shorter histories always win.
